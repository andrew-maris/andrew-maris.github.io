Work from my time as a NIF Summer Scholar at Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory, applying gradient-boosted decision trees to a small and heterogeneous set of inertial confinement fusion experiments to predict neutron yield and identify the design variables driving yield degradation. Later published in *Physics of Plasmas* (2023).
