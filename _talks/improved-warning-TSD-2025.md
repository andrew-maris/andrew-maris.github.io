Machine learning is often assumed to trade interpretability for accuracy. This talk argues the opposite is available in the density-limit problem: a closed-form, dimensionless stability boundary learned from a multi-machine database matches the predictive accuracy of a neural network while remaining simple enough to measure and act on in real time.
