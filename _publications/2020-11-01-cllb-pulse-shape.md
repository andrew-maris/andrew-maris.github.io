Work from outside fusion, on radiation detection instrumentation. CLLB scintillators can distinguish neutrons from gamma rays by pulse shape, but that discrimination is not uniform across the crystal. This study characterizes how performance depends on where in the monolithic crystal the interaction occurs.

2020 IEEE Nuclear Science Symposium and Medical Imaging Conference (NSS/MIC).
