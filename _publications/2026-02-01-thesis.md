My doctoral thesis, submitted to the MIT Department of Nuclear Science and Engineering in February 2026, advised by Cristina Rea, Robert Granetz, and Earl Marmar.

It brings together the multi-machine density limit database, the interpretable machine learning analysis that identified the edge collisionality boundary, and the DIII-D experiments demonstrating real-time avoidance by feedback on machine-learned risk metrics.
